report_problem

Problem

Bosch HR ran pre-onboarding manually across email chains, document attachments, and manual portal entries — a process that spanned 7 days due to back-and-forth coordination, missing documents, and delayed system registrations.

build

Solution

Designed a Power Automate flow triggered by incoming candidate emails. The flow extracts attachments, validates document completeness, saves files to SharePoint with structured naming, registers the candidate in the .NET HR portal, and sends status notifications to HR and the candidate.

emoji_events

Result

Pre-onboarding cycle reduced from 7 to 4 days (43% reduction). 100% document capture rate — no more missing files. Consistent process execution regardless of HR team availability.
