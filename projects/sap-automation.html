The Problem

The finance team at Bosch spent 3+ hours daily manually opening SAP, running transactions, exporting Excel reports, renaming files, and uploading them via FTP. The process was undocumented, error-prone, and blocked by SAP access queues shared across multiple users.

The Solution

A fully automated pipeline that runs daily at 06:00 UTC. The UiPath bot logs into SAP, injects the parameters, extracts the ALV reports, converts them to Parquet, and uploads them to an AWS S3 data lake — without any human intervention.
